Code lives on GitHub: https://github.com/overpressure. Changes come as pull requests; GOVERNANCE.md says who reviews what, CONTRIBUTING.md how.

**Larger changes start with a word here or an issue, before the code**: a new feature or component, and any change to a headset's maintenance system, the installer's writes or the headset interface. A pull request that arrives without that and doesn't fit the project's direction is closed without a review. Fixes and small changes need no announcement.

Want to bring another headset? Open an issue in overpressure/overpressure with the headset, its SoC and where your work is today. Decisions are made in issues and pull requests, not in chat: link the issue when you discuss one here.
