Welcome to overpressure! We run Linux and a SteamOS-compatible userspace on standalone VR headsets, on Valve's Steam Frame kernel. The PICO 4 is the first one.

- **Start:** the installer is at https://github.com/overpressure/overpressure/releases
- **Help:** #overpressure-help:matrix.org, one thread per problem.
- **Bugs and features:** GitHub issues. Chat moves fast; issues are where we track and fix things.
- **Security problems:** report them privately on GitHub (the repository's Security tab), never in chat.

**Rules**

1. Be kind. No harassment, no hate, no personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. English in the shared rooms, so that everyone can follow.
3. Don't post your headset's serial numbers, unlock token, chip ID, or your backup's files. "Save logs…" in the window masks them; read a log before you share it.
4. Don't share Valve's packages, firmware, PICO OS files or built system images. They aren't ours to redistribute.
5. No piracy, no cheats, no ads, no unasked direct messages to people you don't know.
6. Modding a headset voids its warranty and can break it: everything here is at your own risk, as the installer's disclaimer says.

What breaks these rules gets removed. Seeing spam or abuse? Message the admin; questions about a decision too.
